Great question, Arjun! The chain rule is used when you have a composite function. Think of it as peeling layers — differentiate the outer function first, then multiply by the derivative of the inner function.

For f(x) = (3x + 1)², using the chain rule:

1. Outer: u² → 2u

2. Inner: 3x+1 → 3

f'(x) = 2(3x+1) · 3 = 6(3x+1)
